When creating 'PointShadowFrame' for 'PointLightComponent' inside 'ComponentManager', 
the GPU takes the work as pending, then execute when a certain amount of Data (shadow_size * shadow_size) per Face of CubeMap is to be allocated.

That's why a high amount of time is taken during 'Light2' in 'Scene2::init()' approximately 1 second.
